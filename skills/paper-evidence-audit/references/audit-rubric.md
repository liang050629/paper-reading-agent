# Evidence audit rubric

For method claims, require a method definition or mechanism.
For performance claims, require a result with dataset, metric, comparator, and
direction.
For causal claims, require a design capable of causal identification.
For component claims, prefer ablation or controlled comparison.
For efficiency claims, require matched hardware/settings or an explicit caveat.
For generalization claims, inspect coverage across datasets, domains, or
populations.

Flag hidden assumptions, confounds, missing controls, uncertainty omissions,
metric cherry-picking, and conclusions broader than the tested setting.
